✈️ Contrail: Airport Puzzle - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game Contrail: Airport Puzzle (the "App", "we", "our") collects, why, and the choices you have. In short: the App has no accounts, keeps your game progress on your device, and from version 1.1 offers optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward, and the App works fully without them.

Information We Collect

Stored only on your device

  • Game progress: levels completed, stars, Miles, buildings, streaks and settings. This stays on your device and is not uploaded to us.
  • Support ID: a random code created on your device and shown in Settings, so you can quote it if you contact us. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number.

Usage and crash data (you can turn this off)

  • Gameplay analytics: events such as level started, won or lost, moves used and help used, plus device type, operating system, app version, language and approximate country. We use this only to tune level difficulty and improve the game.
  • Crash reports: technical details of a crash (device model, OS version, stack trace) so we can fix bugs.

Both are sent only while the Analytics switch in Settings is on. Turning it off stops collection on that device.

Purchases

If in-app purchases are offered, payments are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or store your card or payment details. The store tells the App which items you own and your store country, so the App can deliver items, restore them and show the right offers.

Rewarded ads (from version 1.1)

The App can offer optional video ads that you choose to watch for an in-game reward (for example a Tow, a Tip or extra Miles). There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt play. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect:

  • Device and ad identifiers: on Android, the device's advertising ID; on iOS, the App does not ask for tracking permission, so the advertising identifier is not available and ads on iOS are not personalised.
  • Ad interaction data: which ads were shown and whether they were watched or tapped, plus approximate location from the IP address, device type and operating system, used to serve the ad, limit how often it is shown, measure it and prevent fraud.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks before any ad data is used, and you can change your choice later in Settings → Privacy and data. Where personalised ads are allowed on Android, you can turn them off at any time by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in the device settings. Rewards never depend on your consent choices: if no ad can be shown, the reward is given for free.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps on iOS (the App does not ask for tracking permission).
  • No user accounts and no chat between players.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config): usage statistics, crash reports and game tuning values. Firebase privacy information
  • Google AdMob: serving and measuring the optional rewarded ads (from version 1.1). How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Apple App Store / Google Play Billing: processing purchases.

These services handle data under their own privacy policies.

Notifications

If you allow them, the App schedules a daily reminder on your device. Reminders are created locally; we do not run a push server or receive any data from them. You can turn them off in the App's Settings or your device settings.

Sharing

When you tap Share, your device's own share sheet opens with a short text and link. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to send it.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the App and data was collected, contact us and we will delete it.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • On your device: Settings → Reset my data erases your progress and Support ID. Uninstalling the App also removes it.
  • Analytics and crash data: kept by Firebase for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically. To have it deleted sooner, email us your Support ID.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete data about you, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent at any time. We do not sell your personal information. On Android, Google AdMob may use the advertising ID to show personalised ads where the law allows it; this can count as "sharing" under some US state laws, and you can opt out by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in the device settings. To use any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

Security

Data sent to Firebase is encrypted in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we collect as little as possible to keep the risk low.

Changes to This Policy

If the App changes what it collects, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date before the change ships.
