What is Contrail?

Contrail is a single-player logic puzzle game for iPhone and iPad, with Android on the way. Each board is a small airport: planes wait on the apron, and a departures board calls them by airline. You tap the plane that is called to send it to the runway, and park the planes that are in its way on stands. Stands fill up, so you have to think about which plane you will need next.

Every takeoff leaves a contrail. Clear the board and the trails join into a glowing picture over the city: a lighthouse cottage in Porto, a hill tram in Lisbon, a puffin over Reykjavik, a paper crane over Kyoto.

How it plays

  1. Tap the called plane. It taxis to the runway and takes off.
  2. Park what is in the way. A plane that is not called goes to a stand. The last plane parked is the first to leave.
  3. Don't box yourself in. Stands fill up and jumbos take two slots. Plan a move or two ahead.
  4. Stuck? Call the Tow. The tug lifts the blocking plane out and you carry on.

Questions

What kind of game is Contrail?

Contrail is a single-player logic puzzle set at an airport. A departures board calls planes by airline. You tap the called plane to send it to the runway and park the planes that are in the way on stands. Every takeoff draws one line, and a cleared board shows a finished picture in the sky.

Is there a timer or a move limit?

No. There are no timers and no move limits. You can think about a board for as long as you like.

Is Contrail free?

Yes. Contrail is free to download and play.

Which devices does it run on?

Contrail runs on iPhone and iPad and is available on the App Store. The Android version is coming to Google Play.

How much is there to play?

There are 300 boards across 15 cities, each with its own sky picture, plus Daily Departures: three new boards every day that are the same for every player.

Is it colorblind-friendly?

Yes. Contrail has colorblind patterns, a shape badge on every plane, a left-handed mode, larger text and a reduced-motion setting.

Which languages does it support?

English, German, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Indonesian and Hebrew.

What is Lost & Found?

Lost & Found is a mini-game that opens when you build the terminal on your airfield. You X-ray bags to find the toys hidden inside and return them to fill your shelves for rewards.
